# Sensor-free demo

`advio-02.mp4` and `advio-02.json` are the first 60 s of the ADVIO
sequence advio-02 (iPhone), as `tools/make_advio_demo.py` writes them: the
portrait video at 30 fps, 360 px wide, and its frame times, IMU samples
resampled to 60 Hz and the camera rig. The page plays them through the VIO
when "Watch a demo" is pressed, and credits the source while it plays.

They are derived from ADVIO and keep its license, CC BY-NC 4.0
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>), not the repository's:

> S. Cortés, A. Solin, E. Rahtu and J. Kannala, "ADVIO: An authentic dataset
> for visual-inertial odometry", ECCV 2018.
> <https://github.com/AaltoVision/ADVIO>
