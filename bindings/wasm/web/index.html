Live visual-inertial odometry, in your browser

This page runs visloc-rs's Rust port of Basalt's monocular-inertial VIO, compiled to WebAssembly, on your phone's camera and motion sensors. It tracks the phone in metric 3D. The camera images stay on the phone.

  • Hold the phone upright, in portrait.
  • To start, move it side to side (half a metre or so) while pointing at a textured scene. A static start can't recover scale.
  • Then walk around. The cube stays put in the world (tap the view to move it in front of you), and the map shows your path.
  • IMU only ignores the camera: the map freezes and moves by the motion sensors alone. Points that stay on their features while you turn the phone mean the camera and IMU agree.

Calibration is loaded for your phone model where known (focal length from the manufacturer's 35 mm-equivalent figure). Tune it under ⚙ if tracking drifts. Uses wasm threads when the page is cross-origin isolated. Served by serve.py, the page sends it diagnostics (timings, counters, errors and phone model; no images); ?telemetry=0 turns that off.
