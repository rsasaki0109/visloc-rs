visloc-rs — photos to 3D, in your browser

visloc-rs's SfM compiled to WebAssembly: SIFT, matching and two-view verification, then the COLMAP-port incremental mapper. SIFT and matching run on the GPU through WebGPU where the browser has it. It all runs on this device; the photos are not uploaded.

Matching and verification
